How You Can Contribute to Your Community

Adding or updating a listing is the simplest way to make the directory more useful for everyone in your town. First, open the web app on your phone or computer and locate the “Suggest a Listing” button, usually found at the top right of the search bar or on the homepage. Clicking it opens a short form that asks for the business name, phone number, email or website, physical address, and a brief list of services offered — for example, “licensed electrician, panel upgrades, wiring repairs.” If you are updating an existing entry, start by searching for the business, open its listing page, and select the “Edit this listing” link that appears beneath the contact information. The edit form pre‑fills the current details; you can then change any field, such as adjusting opening hours from 9 a.m.–5 p.m. to 8 a.m.–6 p.m., adding a new service like “smart‑home installation,” or correcting a typo in the address.

After you submit the form, a local volunteer administrator receives an email notification. They verify the information — typically by checking the business’s website, calling the listed phone number, or confirming with a known resident — and either approve the change or request clarification. In our pilot, most submissions are reviewed within 24 hours, and approved edits appear live on the site immediately after verification. By contributing even a single update — say, adding a weekend‑only childcare provider you recently used — you help neighbors avoid wasted trips and unreliable contacts, and you reinforce the community‑owned nature of the resource hub. Repeating this process whenever you notice a missing or outdated entry keeps the directory current and builds a habit of mutual support that benefits every resident.

Start Finding and Sharing Trusted Services Today

Start by opening the hub on your phone or computer and locating the search bar at the top of the page. Type the service you need — for example, “plumber” — and press enter. If you know a reliable service that isn’t yet in the directory, use the submission form linked in the site’s footer. Enter the business name, contact details, and the services they offer, then hit submit. A local volunteer administrator will verify the information before it appears live, ensuring the hub stays accurate and trustworthy.

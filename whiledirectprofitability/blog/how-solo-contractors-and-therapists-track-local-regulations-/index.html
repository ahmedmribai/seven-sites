The Real Cost of Missing a Local Business or Industry Deadline

A missed NYC contractor permit renewal could mean a $500 fine, but the damage starts earlier: digging through emails to find the last renewal confirmation, cross-referencing it with the city’s website to confirm validity, and then manually logging it in a spreadsheet - all while juggling client work. The same chaos applies to therapists tracking HIPAA deadlines or accountants renewing state CPA licenses. Each deadline requires a separate search, a separate verification, and a separate note in whatever system you’re using (Slack, Notion, or even sticky notes). The cumulative effect? A compliance tracking system that’s as fragile as the last time you moved your laptop.

Worse, if you manually log evidence (like a screenshot of your license renewal), there’s no way to tie it back to the alert, leaving you scrambling during an audit.

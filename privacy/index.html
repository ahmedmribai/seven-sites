Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you ask for a free preview or join a product's list.
  • Order details from Lemon Squeezy when you buy: your name, email, the product and the amount. We never see your full card details.
  • Anonymous visit counts. Product pages store a random ID in your browser's local storage (sv_uid) and tell our server which page was opened. That ID is not linked to your name or email, and our server does not keep request logs or IP addresses for it.
  • Messages you send us.

How we use it

We use it to send the preview you asked for, deliver what you bought, reply to you, and see which products people visit. We do not sell your data, show ads or use third-party trackers. Messages you send may be processed with AI tools, including third-party AI model providers, to help us read and answer them.

Who processes it for us

  • Lemon Squeezy: payments and receipts
  • Resend: sending email
  • Cloudflare: DNS and routing email to our inbox
  • GitHub Pages: hosting these pages. GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

Cookies

We do not set cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is the random visit ID described above, which you can remove by clearing this site's data.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to stop our emails, reply to any of them or write to hello@get-agents-kit.com. We act on requests within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.

Getting started: connect your Shopify store and see filtered alerts today

To get started, open the service’s sign‑up page and create an account with a work email address and a strong password; after submitting the form you will receive a verification link that you must click to activate the account. Once verified, log in and you are taken to the onboarding screen where the first field asks for your Shopify store’s base URL—for example, mystore.myshopify.com. Enter the URL exactly as it appears in your browser’s address bar and press the “Connect Store” button.

The application then initiates Shopify’s OAuth flow: a new tab opens prompting you to log into your Shopify admin if you aren’t already, and to review the requested permissions. The integration only needs read‑access to the Admin API resources that vulnerability scanners typically query—such as Products, Themes, ScriptTags, and Webhooks—so you can grant those scopes without exposing write capabilities. After you click “Install app,” Shopify redirects you back to the service, carrying a short‑lived token that the backend exchanges for a permanent access token stored securely on the server.

With the connection established, the backend pulls the most recent alert feed from whatever scanner you already have configured (e.g., Dependabot, Snyk, or a custom CI job) and applies the Shopify‑specific heuristics. Within a few seconds the dashboard refreshes, presenting a table of alerts sorted by a priority score that reflects the likelihood of being a false positive. Each row shows the scanner’s original severity, a brief description, the affected file or snippet, and a toggle labeled “Mark as False Positive.”

For instance, an alert flagging a missing Content‑Security‑Policy header in a theme snippet that is only loaded in the Shopify admin preview will appear with a low priority score and a pre‑checked false‑positive indicator. Clicking the toggle records your feedback, which the heuristic engine uses to refine future rankings for your store. At this point you can begin reviewing only the high‑priority items, confident that the bulk of benign noise has already been deprioritized, and you can close the loop by marking additional alerts as they appear. The entire process—from sign‑up to viewing the first filtered list—takes under five minutes and requires no changes to your existing scanning pipeline.

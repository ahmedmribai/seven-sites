About Agents Kit

Agents Kit is an experiment in running a small product studio with AI. An AI system we call Seven reads what people say is frustrating them online, proposes small products that could help, builds them, and publishes them here.

It is not left to run unchecked. Every product has to pass an automated substance check before its page goes up, claims on a page are checked against the product itself, and a person reviews anything that charges you money or contacts anyone. Products that nobody finds useful are taken down.

We would rather ship fewer things that work well than many that do not, so each product page says exactly what you get before you pay.
